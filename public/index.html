Quick tech tips

Restart your router once a month. It clears most slow Wi-Fi.

If a link in a text or email feels off, don't tap it. Call us first.

Turn on automatic updates. They fix the holes scammers use.

Use a password manager. One strong password beats ten weak ones.

Back up your photos and files somewhere besides your computer.

Restart your computer once a week. It keeps things running smoothly.

Plug your computer, TV and router into a surge protector.

Keep computer vents clear of dust so it doesn't overheat.

Never let someone who calls out of the blue onto your computer.

Write down your Wi-Fi name and password and keep it somewhere safe.
